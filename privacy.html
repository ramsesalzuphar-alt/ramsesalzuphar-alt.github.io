Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Clipbot is a private desktop application. It is used only by its owner to upload short video clips to YouTube channels and TikTok accounts the owner controls. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Clipbot accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): Clipbot asks only for the youtube.upload permission. It uses it only to upload videos to the owner's own channels. It does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, subscriber lists or any other user information.
  • TikTok: if it is connected, Clipbot asks only for user.info.basic and video.upload, and uses them only to upload videos to the owner's account.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than Google's and TikTok's own APIs. Clipbot has no server or database outside that computer, and no analytics.

Sharing

Clipbot does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Revoking access

You can remove Clipbot's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token files on the computer also removes access.

Third-party terms

By using Clipbot's YouTube features, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Clipbot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy
